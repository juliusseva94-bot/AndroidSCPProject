**SCP Data API**, https://scp-data.tedivm.com/ (unofficial data crawler), derived from the **SCP Wiki**, https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/ . All supplied metadata, extracted text and derived SCP descriptions are subject to their upstream rights and relevant author attributions.
